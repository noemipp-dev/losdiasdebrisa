## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for an imaginative, early-childhood publishing collection featuring coloring books, tactile learning materials, and joyful activities. The design language bridges two core audiences: toddlers/early readers exploring physical pages with tactile delight, and parents or educators seeking inspiring, high-trust educational content.

The emotional signature is **radiant, gentle, tactile, and warmly inviting**. Visual storytelling relies on squishy, toy-like forms, sticker cutouts with layered offsets, and buoyant micro-interactions that evoke storybook pages, sunny meadows, and floating summer clouds. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Tactile / Toy-Box Neomorphism & Playful Pop**:
- Ultra-plump border radii (pill forms and pebble contours).
- Cheerful, candy-like tactile buttons featuring dual-tier press depths and bouncy reactive physics.
- Decal stickers with thick cloud-white outlines and pigmented drop shadows.
- Gentle skyward gradients and creamy canvas backings, eliminating sterile grays in favor of warm buttermilk and light-infused skies.

## Colors

The color palette captures childhood optimism through vibrant candy tones balanced over soft, organic backdrops.

### Primary: Playful Rose
- **Primary Base (`#FF437E`)**: The leading brand mark, primary interactive callouts, celebratory badges, and playful focus points.
- **Primary Dark (`#E11D5B`)**: Active press states, 3D underside bevels, and emphasized headline accents.
- **Primary Tint (`#FFE4EC`)**: Soft tint backplates for cards and hover glow fields.

### Secondary: Sky Blue Breeze
- **Secondary Base (`#0EA5E9`)**: Informational cards, downloadable assets, reading age badges, and sky gradients.
- **Secondary Dark (`#0284C7`)**: Press shadows for blue interactive elements.
- **Secondary Soft (`#E0F2FE`)**: Hero section backdrops, subtle canvas shading, and cloud borders.

### Accents
- **Sunburst Amber (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`)**: Star counters, milestone rewards, interactive chips, and creative prompt alerts.
- **Meadow Green (`#10B981` / `#34D399`)**: Success states, physical book availability chips, nature activity tracks.
- **Lilac Dream (`#8B5CF6` / `#C084FC`)**: Fantasy stories, coloring tool selectors, creative workshop sections.

### Canvas & Text Neutrals
- **Canvas Cream (`#FFFDF5`)**: The core background tone replacing harsh stark white to protect young eyes during reading.
- **Surface Cloud (`#FFFFFF`)**: Raised card surfaces, floating sticker borders, and interactive surfaces.
- **Text Deep Slate (`#1E293B`)**: High-contrast, gentle charcoal for reading passages without black-point harshness.
- **Text Muted Slate (`#64748B`)**: Secondary metadata, author credits, and guide hints.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility, warm curved terminals, and open counters suitable for early literacy and comfortable adult browsing.

- **Display & Headline Font (`Quicksand`)**: Geometric yet deeply rounded, resembling smooth river stones and clean signboards. Generous apertures keep headline lettering friendly, approachable, and effortlessly legible.
- **Body & Continuous Text (`Nunito Sans`)**: Highly structured humanist design with softened stroke caps. Retains reading cadence for longer parent guides, educational curricula descriptions, and book blurbs without feeling childlike or disorganized.
- **Label & Interactive Font (`Quicksand`)**: Heavy weights (`600` and `700`) used across buttons, age badges, pricing pills, and navigation tabs ensure high clarity on colorful backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adopts a **chunky, breathing fluid-grid system** bounded by a standard maximum width of 1200px. Content elements maintain generous padding to prevent sensory clutter and encourage exploratory interaction.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column grid with 24px gutters and 48px page margins. Components enjoy expansive breathing space, with hero sections and book carousels floating over sky-tinted fluid bands.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px page margins. Grids collapse into 2-column card structures for interactive activity books.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 16px lateral padding. Interactive touch targets are enlarged to a minimum touch bounding box of 48px × 48px to accommodate both clumsy child fingers and quick parent navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through **toy-like dimensional planes and sticker reliefs**, completely avoiding harsh, corporate black shadows.

1. **Sticker Outline Depth**: Floating activity elements, book covers, and character badges feature a 3px to 4px pure white perimeter (`#FFFFFF`) backed by a tinted drop shadow (`0 8px 20px rgba(255, 67, 126, 0.18)` or matching accent hue).
2. **Tactile Push Depth**: Interactive buttons possess a pseudo-3D bottom ledge generated via solid box-shadow offsets (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #E11D5B`). On active press, the offset reduces to `0 1px 0` while translating the element downward by 3px, emulating a physical arcade or toy button.
3. **Cloud Elevation (Floating Surfaces)**: Main content cards rest on soft, ultra-diffused atmospheric blurs:
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`
4. **Modals & Overlays**: Light blue veil backdrops (`rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) instead of dark gloomy overlays, sustaining the bright open-sky mood.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces **full pill contours and pebble rounds** (Category 3). Sharp 90-degree corners are strictly prohibited across all interactive surfaces, frames, and cards.

- **Interactive Buttons & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Pebble curvature with `border-radius: 28px` to `36px`.
- **Form Inputs & Search Fields**: Enclosed within comfortable pill capsules (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Illustrative Cutouts**: Asymmetric organic cloud curves (`border-radius: 32px 48px 32px 40px`) used for special promotional badges, reading tips, and character speech bubbles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill (Rose)**: `#FF437E` background with `#FFFFFF` bold text, bottom bevel shadow `0 4px 0 #E11D5B`. Hover triggers slight scale (`transform: scale(1.03)`), active press drops `transform: translateY(3px)` with bevel shadow collapsing to `0 1px 0`.
- **Secondary Pill (Sunburst & Meadow)**: Same physical 3D mechanics using `#F59E0B` (shadow `#D97706`) for shopping / bundle downloads, and `#10B981` (shadow `#059669`) for interactive previews.
- **Ghost Cloud**: Pure white surface, 2px border in `#E0F2FE`, text in `#0EA5E9`. Hover introduces a soft sky blue glow.

### Cards (Book & Activity Tiles)
- **Base Card**: White backdrop (`#FFFFFF`), `border-radius: 32px`, padded with 24px, supported by low-intensity tinted drop shadow.
- **Decorative Accents**: Subtle floating vector ornaments in the corners (small 4-point golden stars `#FBBF24`, mini hearts, or stylized puffy clouds).
- **Book Cover Display**: Book covers are presented tilted at 2 degrees with rounded corners (`16px`), framed in a 4px white sticker border and casting a soft directional shadow.

### Chips & Age Badges
- **Format**: Pill-shaped capsules (`height: 32px`, `padding: 0 16px`).
- **Styling**: Pastel fills matched with saturated text (e.g., Lavender tint `#F3E8FF` with Violet text `#8B5CF6` for "3-5 Años"; Sky tint `#E0F2FE` with Blue text `#0284C7` for "Colorear").

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Form Fields**: Pill contours (`border-radius: 9999px`), 52px height for comfortable touch, warm off-white fill (`#FFFDF5`), 2px soft sky border (`#BAE6FD`). 
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to Vibrant Sky Blue (`#0EA5E9`) with an outer ring glow of `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`. Includes rounded magnifying glass action button embedded inside the right track.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox**: `26px × 26px` squircle shape (`border-radius: 8px`), 2px meadow green border. Checked state fills with `#10B981` and reveals a plump white checkmark icon with a playful spring animation.
- **Radio Button**: `28px × 28px` pill circle. Selected state shows a radiant amber center dot surrounded by a generous white gap.

### Lists & Activity Milestones
- **Progress Trackers**: Segmented caterpillar or cloud paths where completed activities glow with filled radiant colors and celebratory micro-star icons.
- **Content Item Lists**: Spaced row cards with soft rounded dividers, leading with chunky thematic icons encased in colored pebble circles.

### Specialized Components
- **Sticker Ribbon**: Angled badge across book corners with jagged or scallop-stitched edges displaying tags such as "¡Nuevo!", "Imprimible", or "Más Vendido".
- **Floating Cloud Banner**: Alert messages and parent notices nestled in billowing, cloud-contoured pill containers with soft sky blue borders.
